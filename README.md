# DogSnapshot
A client-side mode that saves down your Doggy Talents Next dog as an NBT file on your end, allowing world-to-world transfer.
Ever playing on a server with Doggy Talents Next, grow attached to your dog, happy memories... all suddenly gone with a server shutdown? This mod will save down your dog's NBT file and live on, allowing you to transfer it to wherever you like, as long as the world/server has Doggy Talents Next!

== WHY DID I MAKE THIS MOD? ==

So basically, I have a poodle named Coffee, not a real dog, but a Doggy Talents dog.

I grew quite fond of him, and suddenly the server hosting him closed down, he's gone forever.

I suddenly had the idea for this mod as I was developing the All-In-One Thoroughbred Raising mod, what if instead of saving horses on your end, we move on to Doggy Talent dogs?

That's the story, and that's why the mod's logo is a picture of a DT poodle.

== HOW DO I SAVE MY DOG? ==

Simple, just type in `/tb save [dog name]` to save your own dog in your computer. The files are located in `Users/OWNER/.dogsnapshot/dogs` directory. To summon your dog in another world, type in `/tb load [dog name]`. If two dogs with the same name are saved, one of them will have a random string behind their name in their NBT file. Temporary history NBT files are also kept so you can rollback your dog to its last saved NBTs with the `/tb restore [dog name] [steps (up to 5)]` in case things go wrong.

Creative Mode-edited dogs are flagged, meaning you can only summon that dog if you're an admin in another world.
